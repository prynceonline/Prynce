# ACTIVE — Privacy and data handling (draft)

ACTIVE is intended to provide a public catalogue of apps and approved free downloads. This draft must be reviewed and completed with real operator contact details before public launch.

## Data minimisation

- Public catalogue browsing should not require an account.
- Do not collect precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs or unrelated device identifiers for ordinary catalogue browsing.
- If developer accounts are introduced, collect only information needed to authenticate developers, contact them about submissions and maintain release provenance.
- Do not sell personal data or use app submissions as permission to publish personal information.

## App permissions and privacy disclosures

Each app listing must link to its own privacy policy and explain sensitive permissions and data handling. ACTIVE's review process does not replace the app developer's legal obligations.

## Security

Uploads must be quarantined, scanned, reviewed, checksummed and signature-verified before publication. Provide a way to report suspicious apps and request removal.

## Contact

A real privacy and security contact address must be published before ACTIVE accepts public developer submissions.
